How to read the menu matrix

Items are compared against averages inside the current menu dataset.

  • Star: higher unit sales and higher contribution.
  • Plowhorse: higher unit sales, lower contribution.
  • Puzzle: lower unit sales, higher contribution.
  • Dog: lower unit sales and lower contribution.

These are relative labels for discussion, not universal benchmarks. Review seasonality, portion sizes, waste, taxes, labour and channel fees separately.

Calculation logic

  • Contribution / unit = selling price − ingredient cost − packaging cost.
  • Food cost % = ingredient cost ÷ selling price.
  • Contribution total = contribution / unit × units sold.
  • Revenue = selling price × units sold.

Fixed costs and tax are excluded. Ensure all values refer to the same currency and analysis period.
