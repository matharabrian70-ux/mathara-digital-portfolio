03 / Guided onboarding

From your website URL to a reviewed ordering setup.

Our planned Website-to-Ordering Bridge is designed to remove repetitive setup work while keeping the restaurant in control of its menu, prices and website.

Important: A public website URL can help prepare a draft. It does not authorize software to change the website. The owner must review the extracted information and explicitly approve and authorize the chosen integration.
STEP 01 / DISCOVER

Share your public website URL

The system checks permitted public pages and publicly available menu information. No login or website password is requested.

STEP 02 / PREPARE

Generate a draft profile and menu

Business details, menu items, options and images are organized into a draft with source references and uncertainty flags.

STEP 03 / APPROVE

Review every item and price

The restaurant confirms item names, prices, modifiers, taxes, delivery zones, opening hours and any missing information.

STEP 04 / AUTHORIZE

Connect the chosen integration

The business approves an available embed, OAuth/API, plugin or DNS workflow. The system does not bypass website controls.

STEP 05 / VERIFY

Test before going live

We check the menu, mobile customer flow, order routing, staff access, notifications and payment configuration before production launch.

STEP 06 / HAND OVER

Give the owner operational control

Owners receive access, documentation, support scope and a record of what was configured and tested.

04 / How we deliver

Clear stages. Verified outcomes.

We separate a product concept from a working demo, a working demo from production, and a payment event from a successfully completed onboarding.
