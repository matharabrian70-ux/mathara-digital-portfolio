02 / A little about me

Curious by nature.
Practical by design.

I’m learning by building — testing ideas against real constraints, improving what breaks, and turning complex requirements into clear experiences.

I'm Brian Mathara, a Kenya-based web developer and Computer Science student. My interests sit at the intersection of web development, product design, business systems and automation.

Rather than only making static pages, I enjoy thinking about the complete journey: what a visitor needs to understand, what a customer needs to do, how an operator manages the work, and how the system can stay reliable as it grows.

That mindset has shaped my work on a restaurant ordering platform, hospitality website concepts, and early-stage automation projects. I've learned to treat deployment, integrations, security, mobile usability and clear documentation as part of building — not things to leave until the end.

I'm still growing my experience, and I’m intentional about being transparent: the projects shown here include demos and work in progress, not claims of client results I can't verify. I welcome opportunities to collaborate, solve a defined problem, and keep improving through real work.

04 / How I work

From a rough idea
to a real test.

I aim to make the path from an idea to something people can try as direct and measurable as possible.

STEP 01

Understand the problem

Define who the product serves, what they need, and what success should look like.

STEP 02

Build the smallest useful version

Prioritize the core flow, accessible design and responsive behaviour before adding complexity.

STEP 03

Test, learn, refine

Check the experience, identify gaps, document limitations and improve based on evidence.
